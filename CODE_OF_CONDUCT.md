# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, document decisions, and protect confidential or security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the project and its contributors.
